FAQ

Questions? We have answers.

How much does a website cost?

Our plans start from ₹3,000. The price depends on what you need. We tell you the full price before we start.

How long does it take?

Usually 7 days after you send us your business details.

Do I need to know technology?

No. Just tell us about your business and send your photos. We do the rest.

Who owns the domain and the website?

You do. The domain is registered in your name.

Can I change things later?

Yes. Two rounds of changes are included while we build. Later updates are available in our yearly care plan.

What do you need from me?

Your business name, services and prices, address, phone number, a few photos, and any customer reviews you already have.

What if I do not like the first draft?

Tell us what to change. Two rounds of changes are included.
